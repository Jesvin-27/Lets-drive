FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does it cost to learn?

The learner plan is £2.99 a month for theory practice questions, hazard perception clips, practice routes and your lesson forecast. Lessons are paid one at a time at each instructor's own rate.

Do I have to book a block of lessons?

No. You book and pay for one lesson at a time, so you can try a few instructors and stay with the one who suits you.

What happens if I switch instructors?

Your skills record moves with you. Your new instructor sees where you need help before the lesson starts, so no paid time is spent re-assessing you.

How do payments work?

Payments run through Stripe. The money is held until the lesson or shift is complete and confirmed, then it's paid out.

What does it cost instructors?

£20 a month plus 5% per lesson, and the first 3 months are free for early instructors. You set your own hourly rate.

How are drivers checked for driver hire?

Every driver needs a full UK licence, an Enhanced DBS check, proof of insurance and proof of address before they can bid. Clients confirm their policy covers the driver, or add temporary comprehensive cover for the shift.

Where are you launching first?

Within 20 miles of Newcastle-under-Lyme and Stoke-on-Trent. We grow outwards every two months and aim to cover the whole UK within 9 to 12 months.
